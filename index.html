What We Do

Styles We Specialize In

These aren't just aesthetics. They are living traditions, each with its own history, symbolism, and weight. We approach every one with deep respect.

01
Hawaiian

Kakau — the ancient practice of tattooing native to Hawai'i. Geometric bands and natural forms carrying mana, identity, and connection to 'āina.

02
Samoan

Pe'a and malu — among the oldest continuous tattooing traditions on Earth. Dense, precise, and spiritually transformative designs rooted in fa'asamoa.

03
Chamorro

The tattooing tradition of the indigenous Chamorro people of Guam and the Mariana Islands — geometric, bold, and an act of cultural reclamation.

04
Filipino

Batok — the ancient Filipino tattoo tradition practiced by the Kalinga, Ifugao, and others. Warrior marks of courage and cultural memory.

05
Tongan

Tatatau in the Tongan tradition — powerful geometric works that denote rank, lineage, and spiritual connection to the islands of the Pacific.

06
Fijian

Traditional Fijian tattooing — bold, structured patterns that mark identity, social standing, and connection to the Viti island chain.

07
Norse & Celtic

Knotwork, runes, and bold tribal forms from the northern European tradition — carried with the same reverence as Pacific Island styles.

08
Cover-Ups

Transforming regret into art. Our artists specialize in cover-up work that doesn't just hide — it creates something worth wearing for life.
